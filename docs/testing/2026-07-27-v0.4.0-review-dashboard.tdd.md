## User Journeys

1. Review current scored jobs and their persisted native Career Ops A-F
   findings and evidence.
2. Include unscored jobs without manufacturing a score or Profile verdict.
3. Filter jobs and retain filter state after refresh.
4. Select jobs for later materials and retain the selection after restart.
5. Open the authoritative JobsDB role.
6. Confirm one Quick Apply using the JobsDB default CV and no cover letter.
7. Prevent Apply automation, duplicate submissions, and concurrent browser
   application tasks.
8. Install, diagnose, start, and stop a local-only Dashboard.

## Known Boundaries

- No real JobsDB application was submitted during automated tests.
- A real Quick Apply is irreversible and remains gated on the user's explicit
  choice of one test job and confirmation in the Dashboard.
- The test suite reports existing Pydantic validator deprecations,
  TestClient/httpx deprecation, and legacy SQLite ResourceWarnings. They do
  not fail the current release gate and are not introduced as v0.4 product
  behavior.
- The Dashboard does not create tailored CVs, cover letters, or material
  tasks in v0.4.
- The Dashboard does not infer structured per-Profile-requirement verdicts
  that are absent from the native Career Ops result.
