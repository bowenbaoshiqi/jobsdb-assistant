## User Journeys

1. As a job seeker, I can supply one keyword and search JobsDB Hong Kong without entering
   location or other filters.
2. As a job seeker, I receive at most 50 unique jobs after bounded lazy loading.
3. As a reviewer, I can use the complete JD and its Quick Apply/Apply/unknown classification
   in later evaluation stages.
4. As a repeat user, unchanged JDs reuse their snapshots while changed JDs create immutable
   history.
5. As a cautious user, running discovery never submits an application or prints full JD
   content.
